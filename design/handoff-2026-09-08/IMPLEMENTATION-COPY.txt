PURPOSE & PUBLICATION DISCIPLINE
Keep the confidence. Make the commercial story unmistakable.
The current site has a premium visual identity and distinctive voice. The rewrite does not flatten that personality. It changes the hierarchy so a buyer understands, within seconds, what Pixelette Technologies can build, how AI and automation fit, when blockchain is relevant, what evidence exists, and how to start an engagement.
Non-negotiable publication rule
SELL CAPABILITY. PROVE CLAIMS. DO NOT BORROW CREDENTIALS.
No award, accreditation, certification, review score, client logo, project count, country count, performance statistic or case-study result should be published unless Pixelette has evidence for the exact claim and the right to attribute it publicly. Where a governance or certification route sits with Pixelette Certified or an independent body, describe the route openly rather than implying Pixelette Technologies holds that accreditation.

Executive decisions
Keep the current editorial/serif-led visual character, white space and restrained purple/pink palette.
Retain the full existing hero line: “Engineering that ships. Chains that hold. AI built into both.” Use it as the brand manifesto, not as a 50/50 service taxonomy.
Reweight the proposition: Software Engineering and AI & Automation are the primary commercial engines; Blockchain remains a deep specialist practice rather than half of the first screen.
Give AI & Automation a visible buying route while preserving the principle that AI is engineered into real products and workflows rather than sold as a decorative add-on.
Organise the homepage around customer problems before internal service categories.
Use three clear mental models: BUILD, AUTOMATE, DECENTRALISE, with RUN & IMPROVE beneath all three.
Move selected work and evidence materially higher. Three case-study narratives are supplied later in this document.
Rewrite the Pixelette Certified cross-sell in accreditation-safe language: Technologies engineers; Certified helps clients govern, evidence and prepare for independent assurance where required.
Hold all unverified trust badges and statistics behind an internal claims register.

08  How we deliver
Discover. Design. Build. Verify. Launch. Improve.

Define the business problem, users, systems, data, constraints, risks and success measures. Decide what should — and should not — be built.

Architecture, UX, data model, workflow and delivery plan. For AI, define the baseline and evaluation method before model selection.

Functional testing, security review, performance, model evaluation, human-control points and production-readiness evidence.
05  LAUNCH
Deploy with observability, rollback, monitoring and agreed ownership. Production is treated as an operating state, not a demo.
06  RUN & IMPROVE
Support, incident handling, optimisation, releases and roadmap delivery based on real usage and measured outcomes.

HOMEPAGE FAQS
Questions worth answering before a sales call
What does Pixelette Technologies do?
We design, build, integrate and operate custom software, AI-powered products, automation and specialist blockchain systems. Engagements can start with discovery or with an existing product, process or codebase.
Do you build AI agents and agentic workflows?
We scope and build AI-agent and workflow-automation solutions where the process, data, controls and expected value justify them. More autonomous operation is introduced according to risk and measurable performance, rather than as a default.
Can you add AI to an existing product or system?
Yes. A common engagement is to integrate model or agent capabilities into an existing application, knowledge base or workflow while preserving the systems and controls already in place.
Do you build mobile applications?
Yes. Mobile can be a standalone product or part of a wider SaaS/platform programme across iOS, Android and web, with the architecture chosen around the product and operating requirements.
Can you take over an existing or stalled build?
Yes. We can start with an independent technical assessment, architecture/code review and recovery plan before committing to continued development.
Do you provide developers by the day?
We can structure dedicated engineering capacity where that is the right commercial model, but our default proposition is accountable delivery around a defined product, workflow or engineering outcome.
What happens after launch?
Support can continue through monitoring, incident response, optimisation, releases, roadmap delivery and managed product engineering.
Can you help with compliance or certification?
Where a project needs governance, certification readiness or assurance support, Pixelette Certified can help scope and coordinate the appropriate readiness and independent-assessment route. The exact assurance provider depends on the requirement.
